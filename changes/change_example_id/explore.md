# Explore: <change idea>

## Problem
What is wrong, for whom, and how we know. Evidence: `file.ts:LINE`, a failing
test, a measurement, a client requirement.

## Context
What already exists that touches this. Which capabilities in `specs/` are
involved. What constraints apply — the non-negotiable properties in
`specs/project.md`, the stack, delivery rules.

## Options
At least two, honestly stated. For each: sketch, cost, risk, what it forecloses.
Include "do nothing" and say what it costs.

## Recommendation
One option, with the reason. Name the trade-off you are accepting.

## Open questions
What we could not resolve here and who decides.
